Privacy Policy for Anish

Last updated: 26 June 2026

Anish ("we", "our", or "the app") is a daily mood check-in app. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your choices. We've tried to keep it plain and honest.

Who we are

Anish is an independent app created by Jack D'Arcy. If you have any questions about this policy or your data, contact: jackpatrickdarcy@gmail.com.

What information we collect

When you use Anish, we collect:

  • Account information — the email address you sign up with, and the username you choose.
  • Your mood posts — the emoji, intensity, optional caption, and optional location you choose to share when you post.
  • Friend connections — the friend requests you send and accept, so we can show you your friends' posts.
  • Device push token — a token that lets us send you the daily notification. This identifies your device for notifications only.

We do not collect your precise GPS location automatically. Location is only included if you type it into the optional location field yourself.

How we use your information

We use your information solely to provide the app's features:

  • To create and manage your account and log you in.
  • To show your mood posts to the friends you've connected with.
  • To send you the daily push notification prompting you to check in.
  • To operate the friend system (requests, acceptances, your feed).

We do not sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it for advertising.

Where your data is stored

Your data is stored using Supabase, a third-party backend and database provider, on secure servers. Push notifications are delivered via Expo's push notification service. These providers process your data only to provide their services to the app.

Who can see your mood posts

Your mood posts are visible only to people you have connected with as accepted friends within the app. They are not public.

Your choices and rights

  • Access and deletion — you can request a copy of your data or ask us to delete your account and associated data by emailing jackpatrickdarcy@gmail.com.
  • Notifications — you can turn off push notifications at any time in your device settings.
  • Location and captions — these are optional; you choose whether to include them with each post.

Children

Anish is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children under this age.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

Contact

For any privacy questions or requests, contact: jackpatrickdarcy@gmail.com
